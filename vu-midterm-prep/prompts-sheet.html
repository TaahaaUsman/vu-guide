15-Minute Method

5 prompts · ChatGPT ka free version, ek kaagaz, ek pen · ek lecture ya handout chapter paste karo

1

Scan · 2 min · pehle 30 sec: jo pata hai kaagaz par likho

Analyze this lecture and divide it into 5–8 major concepts. For each concept, tell me what I absolutely need to understand before moving forward. Do not summarize everything.

2

Understand · 5 min · ek concept, laptop band, bol kar samjhao

Teach me these concepts as a VU student. Start from the prerequisite knowledge I need. Explain each concept using one simple example and one VU/exam-relevant example. Avoid unnecessary detail.

3

Recall · 3 min · jawab apne alfaaz mein

Now test my understanding using active recall. Ask me one question at a time about the concepts above. Do not give me the answer. Wait for my response. After I answer, tell me what I got right, what I missed, and then ask the next question.

4

Test · 3 min · short aur long ka jawab likh kar do

Act as a strict university examiner. Based only on the lecture material, create a short practice test. Mix conceptual, application-based and exam-style questions. Ask one question at a time. Do not reveal answers until I attempt each question.

5

Fix · 2 min · score 5 mein se sheet par likho

Analyze my incorrect answers. Identify the underlying concepts I misunderstood. Group my mistakes by topic and create a 5-minute revision plan focused only on my weak areas.

AI ghalti kar sakta hai. Jawab handout se milao. Ye practice hai, "100% aane wale sawal" nahi.
